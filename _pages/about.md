I turn complex data into measurable business value. I'm **triple-certified in Microsoft Fabric** (PL-300, DP-600, DP-700) and fluent in translating technical complexity into clear, executive-ready insight.

- **Business Intelligence** — Interactive Power BI &amp; Tableau dashboards, DAX modeling, and self-service reporting that replaces manual spreadsheets.
